Privacy Policy

Application: MCP App (QuickBooks)
Operator: 27 Miles Capital LLC
Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Overview

MCP App (QuickBooks) is a private, internal integration operated solely by 27 Miles Capital LLC to connect its own QuickBooks Online company data to its own internal tooling. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any third party or end user.

2. Data Accessed

The application accesses accounting data (e.g. accounts, transactions, journal entries, financial reports) from QuickBooks Online companies that 27 Miles Capital LLC itself owns or manages, via the official Intuit QuickBooks Online API, under authorization granted directly by 27 Miles Capital LLC.

3. Data Storage and Use

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally, on infrastructure controlled by 27 Miles Capital LLC, and are used only to make authorized API calls to QuickBooks Online on behalf of the company that granted access. Data retrieved is used solely for internal accounting, bookkeeping, and financial reporting purposes.

4. Data Sharing

No data accessed through this application is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Data does not leave 27 Miles Capital LLC's own systems except as necessary to communicate with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API.

5. Data Retention

Tokens and any cached data are retained only as long as needed for internal use and are deleted upon request or when access is revoked.

6. Revoking Access

Access can be revoked at any time from the QuickBooks Online company's connected-apps settings (Intuit App Center) or by contacting us at the address below.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to legal@27milescapital.com.
